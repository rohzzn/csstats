CS Stats

Add CS Stats to Steam

Check any CS2 player in seconds: Premier and FACEIT ratings, a cheating verdict, their last matches and best clips, right on their Steam profile. Even from the in-game overlay.

CS Stats is installed and on

Open my Steam profile

Open any player's profile and their stats appear under it.

Add to SteamAdd to Chrome
  1. Click Add to Steam. The store opens in a new window.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  3. Close that window and open any Steam profile. The stats appear under it.

Free. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera GX on your PC.

Use it in-game

In CS2, press Shift + Tab, open the web browser and go to

csstats.rohan.my

Then click Add to Steam. It takes about ten seconds.
